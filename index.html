Relax and unwind in our lodge.

Nestled at the foot of the South Downs, West Sussex, is our luxury holiday lodge, sited on our agricultural family run nursery.

Just a few minutes walk to South Downs and about a 40 minute walk to the beach. We have created a peaceful escape, ideal for anyone that wants to get away from the hustle and bustle of every day life.

  • Sleeps 6: 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, all on one level
  • Private covered hot tub, included in the price
  • Check-in from 3pm, check-out by 10am
  • Dogs: one small, well-behaved dog
  • Beach about 10 minutes by car

A family built upon Italian traditions

We have spent many years renovating our nursery and converting the old packing shed into our new family home after spending many happy years living in Elderflower lodge before converting it into our own holiday let so people can come and enjoy what we have created. Every summer we open the greenhouses to sell our produce to the local community.

Before you book

Worth knowing

On the edge of Ferring, easy to reach.

Our lane leaves the main road, so Asda is a 10-minute walk for supplies. Once you're down the lane, guests tell us they barely notice the road.

Next door to our family nursery.

By day you may see us at work in the greenhouses, and in summer we sell our own produce there. The lodge has its own private, fenced garden.

The beach by car or on foot.

Ferring beach is about 10 minutes by car, or about a 40 minute walk (1.7 miles, mostly flat).

What is provided?
Bedding, towels, bathrobes, beach towels, toiletries, Wi-Fi, a full kitchen with coffee pods, and a welcome hamper with eggs from our hens and produce in season.
Is there parking?
Yes, free: 2 cars at the lodge, with more space on the nursery.
Is it step-free?
Inside is all on one level, with a large wet room and widened doorways. There are 2 steps to the front door; we put a ramp down on request.
How do we get there without a car?
Goring-by-Sea station is 1.5 miles away (about 30 minutes on foot); Angmering station is 1.9 miles.
Can we bring a dog?
Yes. We accept one small, well-behaved dog.
How do we book?
Choose dates and book on Airbnb, or book direct with us: ring or text 07940 796860, or email info@highdownhideaways.com.
7 more questions
Is there Wi-Fi and TV?
Fast Wi-Fi throughout. A 50-inch smart TV in the lounge (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video) and a 40-inch smart TV in every bedroom.
Is there air conditioning?
Not yet. Fans are provided for hot nights.
How many people does it sleep?
Up to 6, in two king-size bedrooms and a twin room. A travel cot and highchair are free on request.
Is the hot tub private? Does it cost extra?
It is private to the lodge, under its own covered gazebo in your garden, and included in the price. You can use it in the rain.
Check-in and check-out?
Check-in from 3pm, check-out by 10am. Self check-in with a lockbox. Ask us if you need other times.
How far is the beach?
About 10 minutes by car, or about 40 minutes on foot (1.7 miles, mostly flat; the shorter footpath over the railway is closed until February 2027).
Parties or smoking?
No parties, events or smoking.
