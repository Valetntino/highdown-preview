Walks from the door

Highdown Hill
Up Hangleton Lane to Highdown Hill (National Trust), about 16 min on foot. The trig point stands 81 m above the sea on a Bronze Age hill fort, with the view over Worthing to the sea. Komoot's published loop from Ferring to the trig point is 5.2 km (3¼ miles), about 1 h 25 with 80 m of climbing.
Ferring beach and Goring Greensward
Flat. The beach is about 40 min on foot. Komoot's loop from Ferring past the Bluebird Cafe and along Goring Greensward is 4.4 km (2¾ miles), about 1 h 07.
Cissbury Ring
The largest hill fort in Sussex, about 20 minutes by car to Storrington Rise car park, Findon Valley (BN14 0HT). The National Trust trail is 2.2 miles, 1 to 1½ hours, with steep slopes, steps and kissing gates; dogs on a lead. The National Park's circular is 3 to 4 miles, 1 to 2 hours, on uneven ground. The Park notes a fallen tree on its route: at the first wooden gate on the right, leave the wooded path.
With a dog
Keep it on a lead near sheep, cattle and horses. Some local routes have stiles that are hard for bigger dogs; no published route counts them.

Sources: Komoot: easy hikes around Ferring, South Downs National Park: Cissbury Ring circular, National Trust: Cissbury Ring

Highdown Gardens

Getting there
27 min on foot or 5 min by car, at the foot of the hill. Free entry, every day of the year.
Opening hours
January and December 10am to 4pm. February, March, October and November 10am to 4.30pm. April and September 10am to 7pm. May and August 10am to 8pm. June and July 10am to 9pm.
Dogs
The gardens welcome assistance dogs on a lead and say nothing about pet dogs, so ring before bringing yours.
Parking and tea
Small car park with an overflow field and 2.1 m height barriers; the pub's car park is not for the gardens. Toilets at the entrance and a tearoom by the top car park. Bad weather can close the gardens at short notice.

Sources: Highdown Gardens: find us

The beaches and their dog rules

Ferring beach
About 40 min on foot or 10 min by car. Dogs are welcome all year: Arun's summer ban (1 May to 30 September) covers parts of Bognor Regis, Felpham and Littlehampton only, with a £100 fine there. The Bluebird Cafe on the beach is dog friendly, open 8am to 5pm every day.
Goring and Worthing
From 1 May to 30 September dogs are banned day and night on two stretches: at Goring between the launch ramp in line with Seafield Avenue and the ramp at Worthing Sailing Club, and at Worthing from Heene Road to Splash Point rocks. Elsewhere keep dogs under control, and on a lead on the promenade.
Coffee and toilets
Sea Lane Cafe on Goring seafront is open daily 8am to 5.30pm, with a free car park and toilets at Marine Crescent.
Safety
No lifeguards. Coastal wardens patrol in summer; the Coastal Office is on 01903 238977.

Sources: Arun District Council: 2026 dog beach ban, Adur and Worthing Councils: beaches, The Bluebird Cafe

Pubs and cafes

The Henty Arms, Ferring Lane
The nearest pub, 15 to 20 min on foot. Dog friendly, family friendly, car park.
The Highdown, Goring
A Brunning & Price pub with a tea room, about 25 minutes on foot. Its website gives no dog policy, so ask.
The Bull, Goring Street
Chef & Brewer. Dogs welcome in parts of the pub and the garden. Book for Sunday lunch.
The World's End, Patching
About 11 minutes by car. Dog friendly: say so when booking and they will seat you to suit. Book for Sunday lunch.
Perch on the Pier, Worthing
Dog friendly, walk-ins welcome. Closed Monday to Wednesday.
The Black Rabbit, Arundel
On the river near the castle, about 25 minutes by car. Dogs welcome. Book ahead.

Sources: The Henty Arms

Wet days and winter

Arundel Castle
The 2026 season runs 1 April to 1 November, closed Mondays except bank holidays and August. Assistance dogs only. 2027 dates are not out yet.
WWT Arundel Wetland Centre
Open daily 10am to 4.30pm (closed Christmas Day). Adult £16.10, family £44.85 (2026 prices), free parking. Assistance dogs only.
Worthing Museum and Art Gallery
Wednesday to Saturday from 10am, until 8pm on Thursdays.
Worthing Pier
Free, open 6am to 10pm in fair weather, with the Pavilion Theatre at the shore end; the Dome Cinema is on Marine Parade nearby. Worthing is 10 to 15 min by car.

Day trips

Brighton
30 to 55 min by car.
Chichester
25 to 45 min by car. The cathedral is open Monday to Saturday 9am to 4.30pm and Sunday 12.30pm to 3pm; a £7 donation is suggested.
Trains
